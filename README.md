# 🏨 Hotel Booking Business Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes hotel booking data using **Excel** and **Power Query** to uncover business opportunities, improve pricing decisions, and reduce revenue loss caused by booking cancellations.

The analysis focuses on answering real business questions rather than creating visualizations only.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project was built around three key business questions:

### 1. Where are bookings coming from?

- Top countries generating bookings
- Top travel agents and distribution channels
- Customer segments with the highest demand
- Dependency on specific markets

---

### 2. When do bookings happen?

- Seasonal booking trends
- Monthly booking patterns
- High and low demand periods
- Opportunities for seasonal promotions

---

### 3. What opportunities are we missing?

- Revenue optimization
- Room pricing opportunities
- Cancellation impact
- Deposit policy recommendations

---

# 🧹 Data Preparation

Data cleaning was performed using **Power Query**.

### Cleaning Process

- Handled missing values without removing valuable records whenever possible.
- Replaced NULL values using logical Power Query transformations.
- Corrected data types.
- Created calculated fields to support business analysis.
- Added data quality checks to highlight problematic records instead of deleting them.

---

# 📊 Key Business Insights

### Distribution Channels

- Identified the highest-performing booking channels.
- Discovered heavy dependency on specific markets.

### Room Performance

- High-demand room categories generated lower margins than expected.
- Suggested reviewing room pricing strategy.

### Seasonality

- Peak demand occurs during Spring and Summer.
- Winter presents an opportunity for promotional campaigns targeting colder European countries.

### Cancellation Analysis

- Most cancelled bookings had **No Deposit**.
- Estimated financial impact of cancellations.
- Recommended introducing deposit policies to reduce revenue loss.

---

# 📈 Business Recommendations

- Reduce dependency on a limited number of markets.
- Reprice high-demand room categories.
- Launch winter promotional packages.
- Introduce booking deposits for selected reservation types.
- Monitor cancellation KPIs continuously.
